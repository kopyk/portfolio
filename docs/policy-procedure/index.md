## The source requirement

The Electronic Fund Transfer Act ([15 U.S.C. 1693 et seq.](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/chapter-41/subchapter-VI)) protects consumers who use electronic transfers such as debit card purchases, ATM withdrawals and direct deposits. The Consumer Financial Protection Bureau issues the rule that implements it: Regulation E, [12 CFR Part 1005](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/part-1005).

[Section 1005.11](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1005.11) says what a financial institution must do when a consumer reports an error with an electronic transfer. In short: investigate promptly, decide within set time limits, fix what is wrong, and tell the consumer the result.
