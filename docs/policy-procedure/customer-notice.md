## If something is wrong with an electronic transfer

*Fernhill Bank sends you this notice when you open your account and once each year. Keep it with your records.*

Electronic transfers include debit card purchases, ATM withdrawals and deposits, direct deposits, and payments you set up to come out of your account.

### Tell us as soon as you can

Contact us if you think your statement or a receipt is wrong, or if you need more information about a transfer.

**We must hear from you no later than 60 days after we sent the first statement that showed the problem.**

- Call us at 1-800-555-0114.
- Send a secure message in online banking.
- Write to Fernhill Bank, Dispute Operations, PO Box 1140, Springfield, ST 00000.

### What to tell us

1. Your name and account number.
2. Which transfer you are unsure about, and its date if you know it.
3. The dollar amount of the suspected error.
4. Why you think there is an error, or what more you need to know. Be as clear as you can.

If you tell us by phone or in a branch, we may ask you to send your question in writing within 10 business days.

### What counts as an error

- A transfer you did not make or approve.
- A transfer for the wrong amount.
- A transfer missing from your statement.
- An ATM that gave you the wrong amount of cash.
- A math or recordkeeping mistake by us.

### What we will do

**We will decide whether there was an error within 10 business days after we hear from you.** If there was, we will correct it within 1 business day.

Sometimes we need more time. If so, we may take up to 45 days. When we take more time, we will put the amount you think is in error back in your account within 10 business days. You can use that money while we finish. We will tell you within 2 business days after we do this.

If we ask you to put your question in writing and do not receive it within 10 business days, we may not put the money back while we investigate.

Some cases can take longer:

| If the transfer | We decide within | Or, if we put the money back first, within |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Happened in the first 30 days after your first deposit | 20 business days | 90 days |
| Was a debit card purchase at a store or online | 10 business days | 90 days |
| Started outside the United States | 10 business days | 90 days |

We will tell you what we found within 3 business days after we finish.

### If we find no error

We will send you a written explanation. You may ask for copies of the documents we used to decide.

If we put money back in your account while we looked into it, we will tell you the date and amount before we take it out again. For 5 business days after we tell you, we will still pay checks and scheduled payments from your account as if the money were there, and we will not charge you an overdraft fee for them.

### A "business day"

A business day is any day our offices are open to the public for normal business.
