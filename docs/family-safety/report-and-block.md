Use this page when another player has upset, scared or harmed your child.

:::danger If your child is in danger right now
Call your local emergency number first. In the United States, call 911. Then come back and report the player.
:::

## What to do first

1. **Stay calm with your child.** Thank them for telling you. They are not in trouble.
2. **Do not reply to the player.**
3. **Block the player.** This stops contact right away.
4. **Report the player.** This sends the case to the safety team.

You do not need to save the chat yourself. Kitewood keeps a copy of the messages when you send a report.

## Block the player

Blocking stops the player from sending messages or friend requests. It also keeps them out of games your child is in. The player is not told who blocked them.

Do these steps on your child's device.

<Steps>

1. Open the game menu, then select **Players**.
2. Select the player's username.

   If the player has left, select **Recent players** to find them.

3. Select **Block**, then select **Block** again to confirm.

</Steps>

## Report the player

You can report from your child's device or from your own.

<Steps>

1. On the player's profile, select **Report**.
2. Select the reason that fits best.

   | Reason | Use it when the player |
   | --- | --- |
   | **Bullying** | Was mean to your child, or left them out on purpose, more than once. |
   | **Asking for personal details** | Asked for a real name, school, address, phone number or photo. |
   | **Unsafe contact** | Asked your child to keep a secret, to meet, or to chat in another app. |
   | **Cheating or scams** | Tried to trick your child out of items or money. |
   | **Something else** | Did something that is not listed. |

3. In the box, write what happened in your own words. One or two lines is enough.
4. Select **Send report**.

</Steps>

Kitewood shows a report number. Write it down.

## What happens next

- The safety team reads your report. Reports about unsafe contact are read first.
- You get an email when the team has made a choice. Most reports get an answer within 2 days.
- The team may warn the player, limit their account or remove it. To protect privacy, the email does not say which.
- The player is never told who sent the report.

## If the player comes back

A blocked player may make a new account. If this happens:

- Block and report the new account. In the box, add your first report number.
- Set Chat to **Off** for a while. See [Change who can chat with your child](./change-chat-settings.md).

## Get more help {#get-more-help}

To reach the safety team, select **Settings**, then **Help**, then **Contact the safety team**. Include your report number if you have one.
