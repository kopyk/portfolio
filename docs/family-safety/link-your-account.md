Link your account to your child's account to open the Family Dashboard. The dashboard is where you set chat, friends, game rating, play time and spending.

**Time:** about 5 minutes.

## Before you start

You need:

- Your own Kitewood account. You must be 18 or older.
- Your child's username.
- Your child and their device with you. Your child must approve the link.

## Send a link request

Do these steps on your own device.

<Steps>

1. Sign in to your Kitewood account.
2. Select **Settings**, then select **Family**.
3. Select **Add a child**.
4. Enter your child's username, then select **Send request**.

   Kitewood shows a 6-digit code. Keep this screen open. The code works for 15 minutes.

</Steps>

## Approve the request

Do these steps on your child's device, with your child.

<Steps>

1. Open Kitewood. A message says that a guardian wants to link accounts.
2. Select **Review request**.
3. Check that the name shown is yours.
4. Enter the 6-digit code from your device, then select **Approve**.

</Steps>

## Set your guardian PIN

Go back to your own device.

<Steps>

1. Enter a 4-digit PIN, then enter it again.
2. Select **Save**.

</Steps>

Kitewood asks for this PIN each time a safety setting is changed. Do not share it with your child.

## Check that it worked

On your device, select **Settings**, then select **Family**. Your child's username is listed under **Linked accounts**.

New links start with the safest choices: Chat is **Off**, and Friend requests is set to **Ask me first**.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Username not found** | Check the spelling with your child. Usernames do not have spaces. |
| **Code expired** | On your device, select **Send request** again to get a new code. |
| No message on your child's device | Close Kitewood on your child's device and open it again. |
| **This account is already linked** | A child account can have two guardians. If you do not know the other guardian, [contact the safety team](./report-and-block.md#get-more-help). |
