## Where to find things

All controls are in one place. On your own account, select **Settings**, then **Family**, then your child's username.

| I want to | Select | Choices |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stop all messages | **Chat** | Off |
| Allow messages from friends | **Chat** | Friends only |
| Approve new friends | **Friend requests** | Ask me first |
| Limit games by age | **Game rating** | All ages, 9 and up |
| Limit daily play | **Play time** | 30 minutes to 4 hours |
| Limit or stop spending | **Spending** | No spending, or a monthly limit |

You need your guardian PIN to save a change.

## If there is a problem

| Problem | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Your child is in danger now | Call your local emergency number. |
| A player is bothering your child | [Block the player, then report them](./report-and-block.md). |
| You forgot your PIN | Select **Forgot PIN** on any PIN screen. |
| You need a person | Select **Settings**, then **Help**, then **Contact the safety team**. |

## Words used in these guides

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Family Dashboard** | The screen where you set your child's controls. |
| **Guardian PIN** | The 4-digit number you enter to save a change. |
| **Linked account** | A child account joined to your guardian account. |
| **Text filter** | The tool that hides unsafe words and personal details in chat. |
| **Safety team** | The Kitewood staff who read reports. |
