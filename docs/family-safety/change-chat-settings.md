Use the Chat control to choose who can send your child messages. You can change it at any time.

**Time:** about 2 minutes.

## Before you start

- [Link your account to your child's account](./link-your-account.md) if you have not done so.
- Have your guardian PIN ready.

## Pick a chat setting

| Setting | Who can send your child messages |
| --- | --- |
| **Off** | No one. Your child can still play games with other players. |
| **Friends only** | Only players on your child's friends list. |

The text filter checks every message with both settings.

## Change the setting

<Steps>

1. Sign in to your Kitewood account.
2. Select **Settings**, then select **Family**.
3. Under **Linked accounts**, select your child's username.
4. Select **Chat**.
5. Select **Off** or **Friends only**.
6. Enter your guardian PIN, then select **Save**.

</Steps>

The change starts right away. Your child does not need to sign out.

## Decide who counts as a friend

**Friends only** is as safe as the friends list. To approve each new friend yourself:

<Steps>

1. On the same screen, select **Friend requests**.
2. Select **Ask me first**.
3. Enter your guardian PIN, then select **Save**.

</Steps>

From now on, each friend request comes to you. You get a notice in Kitewood and by email. Your child cannot chat with the new friend until you approve.

## Check that it worked

Select your child's username again. The **Chat** row shows the setting you picked.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Wrong PIN** | Try again. After 5 wrong tries, Kitewood locks the settings for 15 minutes. |
| You forgot your PIN | Select **Forgot PIN**. Kitewood sends a reset link to your email. |
| Your child still gets messages | The messages may be from before the change. If new ones arrive, [report the player](./report-and-block.md). |
