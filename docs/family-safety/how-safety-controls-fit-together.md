Kitewood has two kinds of safety tools. Some are always on. Others are controls that you set for your child. This page explains both, so you know what each one does before you change it.

## What is always on

These protections apply to every child account. You do not need to set them up, and no one can turn them off.

- **Text filter.** Kitewood hides words and phrases that are not safe for kids. It also hides phone numbers, addresses and other details that could show who your child is.
- **Safety team.** Trained staff read reports from players and guardians.
- **Private profile.** Other players see your child's username and avatar. They do not see a real name, age or location.

## What you control

You set these in the **Family Dashboard**. To use it, first [link your account to your child's account](./link-your-account.md).

| Control | What it does | Choices |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Chat** | Sets who can send your child messages. | Off, Friends only |
| **Friend requests** | Sets whether you must approve new friends. | Ask me first, Allow |
| **Game rating** | Sets which games your child can open. | All ages, 9 and up |
| **Play time** | Sets how long your child can play each day. | No limit, or 30 minutes to 4 hours |
| **Spending** | Sets how much your child can spend each month. | No spending, or a limit you choose |

## How the controls work together

Think of the controls as three questions.

1. **Who can reach my child?** Chat and Friend requests answer this. If Chat is set to **Friends only**, then the friends list decides who can send messages. Set Friend requests to **Ask me first** and you decide who is on that list.
2. **What can my child see?** Game rating answers this. A game above the rating you pick does not open.
3. **How much can my child do?** Play time and Spending answer this.

The always-on tools sit under all three. Even with Chat set to **Friends only**, the text filter still checks each message.

## Who can change a control

Only you can change a control. Your child can see the settings but cannot edit them.

When you link accounts, you pick a **guardian PIN**. Kitewood asks for this PIN each time a setting is changed. Keep the PIN to yourself.

## What your child sees

Kitewood tells your child that a guardian account is linked. It also shows them which controls are on. We do this on purpose. Kids are safer when they know the rules and can talk about them with you.

## What the controls cannot do

No setting can replace a talk with your child. The controls lower risk. They do not remove it. Ask your child to tell you when something online feels wrong. Then use [Report and block a player](./report-and-block.md).
