**Role:** Technical Writer, Gainwell Technologies, February 2025 to February 2026.

## Problem

Vendors that exchange healthcare transactions with a state program have to follow two layers of rules: the national ASC X12 EDI 5010 standards, and the program's own choices within those standards. A companion guide documents the second layer.

## Approach

- **Gathered and reconciled input from more than 20 subject matter experts.** [JORDAN: how? Interviews, review cycles, a tracking sheet? How did you settle conflicts between SMEs?]
- **Wrote to two standards at once.** Every guide had to stay within HIPAA and the ASC X12 EDI 5010 standards. [JORDAN: how did you check a guide against the standard before release?]
- **Broke complex processes into task-based guidance** for readers who were not EDI specialists. [JORDAN: one concrete example of a structural change, described without quoting the guide.]
- **Kept guides current** as workflows changed, through versioned updates. [JORDAN: what triggered an update, and who approved it?]
- **Built a Power BI dashboard to track vendors,** and presented it to more than 15 stakeholders.

## Result

- Six user-facing companion guides for the Washington, D.C. EDI team.
- Vendor onboarding questions fell by about 30%. [JORDAN: how was this measured, and over what period? If you cannot say, cut this prompt and keep the figure as it appears on the resume.]
