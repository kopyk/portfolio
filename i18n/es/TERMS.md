# Term list: English to Spanish (es)

Context for whoever translates or reviews the family safety guides.

- **Audience:** parents and guardians of children under 13. Not technical.
- **Register:** plain and warm. Address the reader as *usted*.
- **Reading level:** short sentences, common words. The English source is held to grade 8 or lower.
- **UI language:** assume the Kitewood product is translated. UI labels are translated and shown in bold, as in the source.
- **Status:** the Spanish quick reference is a draft. A fluent reviewer has not yet checked it.
